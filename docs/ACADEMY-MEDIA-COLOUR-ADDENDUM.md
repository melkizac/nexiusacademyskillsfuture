# Nexius Academy media and co-branding addendum

**Date:** 13 October 2026
**Scope:** Academy website media, campaign assets and represented-organisation marks
**Status:** Adopted for the Academy colour-correction release after preview review on 13 October 2026

This clarification supplements Nexius Brand Identity v1.2. It preserves the supplied full guide and existing Nexius logo masters.

## Neutral identity and authentic assets

Nexius-owned page furniture, typography and current logo masters retain the guide's neutral identity. Photographs and other organisations' logos retain their authentic colours.

Photography should show truthful content and recognisable people, with documentary composition, natural light and restrained grading. Apply greyscale only for a specific reviewed art-direction reason, not automatically to every image.

Representation and partner logos use authentic supplied or official colours at normal opacity. Preserve each mark's proportions, clear space and optical weight on a neutral backing. Do not recolour marks to match an Academy accent, invent colours for an official monochrome mark, or hide colours until hover. Keep the organisations-represented disclaimer visible; appearance does not imply endorsement.

Colours embedded in photographs or third-party marks are asset colours, not new Nexius corporate palette tokens. Semantic UI colours retain their operational meanings. Decorative Academy accents require a stated role and scoped review; previous workshop orange does not automatically expand to every channel.

## Existing campaign art

The workshop poster is retained in its existing blue/cyan colours for this correction. Removing its display filter does not resolve its blue-glow art-direction difference from v1.2. A future artwork refresh is a separate decision and must preserve presenter identity and current event facts, address and registration destination.

## Governance

Keep this dated Academy addendum with the site's design documentation. A future complete identity-pack amendment receives a separate version, matching exports and revision history. Preserve original guides and masters. Coloured Nexius-logo concepts remain concepts until a master treatment is explicitly selected and produced.
